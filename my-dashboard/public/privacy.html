Privacy Policy

RMW Dashboard (dashboard.ritzmediaworld.in) · Last updated 23 October 2026

The RMW Dashboard is an internal work tool for Ritz Media World staff. This page explains what information it handles, including the AI Library feature, which uses Google Drive.

Who can use it

Only staff accounts approved by an administrator can sign in. The dashboard is not offered to the public.

Information we store

  • Your account details: name, work email, department and role.
  • Work records you create in the dashboard, such as tasks, comments and reports.
  • For AI Library uploads, a log entry for each file: the file name, size and type, who uploaded it, which client it was for, the date, and the file's Google Drive link.

Google Drive (AI Library)

An administrator connects one company Google account to the AI Library. Files that staff upload are saved in that account's Google Drive, in an "AI Library" folder organised by date, person and client.

  • The dashboard asks Google only for the drive.file permission. That lets it create and read the files and folders it made itself. It cannot see, read or change any other file in that Drive.
  • Files are sent directly from the uploader's browser to Google Drive. They are not stored on the dashboard's servers.
  • The connection is kept as an encrypted Google authorization token on our server. It is used only to create upload folders, start uploads and confirm that an upload finished.
  • The dashboard's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How information is used and shared

Information is used only to run the dashboard for Ritz Media World's own work. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with third parties, except the service providers that host the dashboard and, for AI Library files, Google Drive.

Keeping and deleting information

Files uploaded through the AI Library stay in the company Google Drive until someone with access to that Drive deletes them. An administrator can disconnect Google Drive at any time from the AI Library page. That revokes the dashboard's access, and the connected account can also remove it from its Google account permissions.

Contact

For questions about this policy or to ask for your information to be corrected or removed, contact the RMW Dashboard administrator at Ritz Media World.
